Privacy Policy

harshithp.me operates with a strict zero-surveillance, privacy-first philosophy. This site does not sell data, track you across the web, or run third-party advertising scripts.

Core Principles

  1. 01
    Zero Third-Party Trackers

    There are no Google Analytics, Facebook pixels, surveillance beacons, or commercial tracking scripts on this domain. Your browsing session stays between your client and this edge server.

  2. 02
    Anonymous First-Party Visitor Counter

    When connecting to the live backend server, an anonymous cryptographic hash (SHA-256) of a random UUID is used solely to deduplicate unique visits and prevent tally inflation. We never log your IP address, browser fingerprint, or geographic location in the visitor registry.

  3. 03
    Local Storage Preferences Only

    Browser local storage is only used to store functional UI preferences: your theme selection (theme: 'light' | 'dark') and the Thing companion toggle state. These values never leave your device.

  4. 04
    Cookies

    The only cookie utilized is an anonymous session token (anon_vid) configured with HttpOnly; SameSite=Lax; Secure. It contains no personally identifiable data and is only read by the first-party counter.

  5. 05
    External Services & Repositories

    Links to third-party services (such as GitHub, LeetCode, CodeChef, Codeforces, X, LinkedIn, Spotify, and GrindLens) navigate to independent platforms with their own privacy policies.
